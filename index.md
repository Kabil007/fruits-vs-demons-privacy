# Privacy Policy

**Fruits vs Demons: Dark War**

**Last updated: October 11, 2026**

F11 Software Lab built Fruits vs Demons: Dark War as a mobile game. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and handled when you use the game.

## 1. Information We Collect

Fruits vs Demons: Dark War does not require you to create an account to play.

The game stores gameplay information locally on your device, such as:

- Level progress
- Coins
- Hero upgrades
- Game settings
- Statistics
- Cosmetic selections
- Other game progress

Third-party services used by the game may collect certain information automatically as described below.

## 2. Advertising

Fruits vs Demons: Dark War uses Google AdMob to provide advertisements.

The game may display:

- Interstitial advertisements at appropriate breaks after gameplay
- Optional rewarded advertisements that players may choose to watch for an in-game benefit

The game does not use banner advertisements.

Google Mobile Ads may process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, diagnostic information, interactions with advertisements, and other information necessary to provide, measure, secure, and improve advertising services.

For more information about how Google handles information, please review Google's privacy policies.

## 3. Consent and Privacy Choices

Where required, the game uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request consent or present privacy choices relating to advertising.

Depending on your region, you may be able to manage applicable advertising privacy choices from within the game.

## 4. Rewarded Advertisements

Rewarded advertisements are optional.

If you lose a battle, the game may offer you the option to watch a rewarded advertisement to receive an in-game revive.

You are not required to watch rewarded advertisements to continue using the game.

## 5. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

Fruits vs Demons: Dark War may offer optional in-app purchases through Google Play, including:

- Coin packs
- Cosmetic content
- Other optional digital content

The game may also offer Remove Ads subscription plans.

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Google Play. F11 Software Lab does not directly receive or store your complete payment-card information.

An active Remove Ads subscription is intended to remove forced interstitial advertisements. Optional rewarded advertisements may remain available if you choose to use them.

## 6. Children's Privacy

F11 Software Lab does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through the game.

Advertising, consent, age-rating, and audience settings will be configured in accordance with applicable Google Play and Google advertising requirements.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information directly to us, please contact us using the information below.

## 7. Local Game Data

Most game progress is stored locally on your device.

Deleting the application or clearing its application data may remove locally stored progress unless a feature specifically provides backup or restoration.

## 8. Internet Access

The core game can be played without a continuous internet connection.

Internet access may be required for features such as:

- Advertisements
- Rewarded advertisements
- In-app purchases
- Subscription verification
- Privacy and consent services

If these online services are unavailable, the core offline campaign is intended to remain playable.

## 9. Third-Party Services

The game may use services provided by third parties, including:

- Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads
- Google User Messaging Platform
- Google Play services
- Google Play Billing

These third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

## 10. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with the game. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 11. Data Retention and Deletion

Game progress stored locally on your device can generally be removed by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the game.

Information processed independently by Google or other third-party services is subject to those providers' respective retention and deletion policies.

If you contact F11 Software Lab directly, we may retain your correspondence as reasonably necessary to respond to your request and maintain appropriate records.

## 12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the game, its services, or applicable requirements change.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
